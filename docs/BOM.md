## Dimensions currently validated

- Fixed tube: 48.0 mm internal diameter, 52.0 mm external diameter
- Rotating part: 52.2 mm internal diameter, 56.0 mm external diameter
- Diametral running clearance between the tubes: 0.2 mm
- Display bezel diameter: approximately 36.4 mm
- Display guide clearance at the top feature: approximately 0.46 mm minimum in CAD

Measurements should be rechecked on the final print before these values are
treated as production dimensions.
